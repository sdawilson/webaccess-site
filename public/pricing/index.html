Questions about the plans

Is the free plan a trial?

No. It doesn't run out. It's for building and testing on your own: one database, up to 5,000 records and 10 MB, with every design tool. A free account that isn't used for 180 days is deleted.

Who needs a paid seat?

Admins: the people who design tables, forms, reports and code. The people you share a database with, Users who work with the data and Guests who only read it, don't pay and don't need a licence of their own.

What counts towards the 5,000 records and 10 MB?

The records in all the tables of your database together, and its size including attachments. The sample database doesn't count.

Why can't I share on the free plan?

Sharing is what the subscription pays for. Preview as User opens your database exactly as your team would see it, so you can finish and test everything before you subscribe.

What if more than 10 people need one database?

A database can move to a hosted SQL database for more people, at a fee that covers its hosting. Get in touch and tell us how many.

Can I take my data away?

Yes, on either plan. Back Up Database downloads a complete copy, and any table exports to Excel or CSV.
