Questions about the plans

Is the free plan a trial?

No. It doesn't run out. It's for building and testing on your own: one database, up to 5,000 records and 10 MB, with every design tool. A free account that isn't used for 180 days is deleted.

Is it really a one-off payment?

Yes. Desktop is £99 and Server is £495, each paid once for each Admin. There's no subscription. With Server, you pay bunny.net for the hosting in your own account.

Who needs a licence?

Admins: the people who design tables, forms, reports and code. The people who use what you build, Users who work with the data and Guests who only read it, need no licence and pay nothing, however many they are.

What's the difference between Desktop and Server?

Where it runs. Desktop runs on your own computer, with your data on it, and shares with other computers on your office network. Server is the web version, on a server of your own, for a team that works from a link in a browser, wherever they are.

When can I buy Desktop or Server?

Soon. Both are being finished and tested. Leave your email and we'll tell you the day each is ready. Meanwhile everything you build on the free plan can be taken with you.

Can I move what I build on the free plan?

Yes. Back Up Database downloads a complete copy of a database, which opens in Desktop or on a Server. Any table also exports to Excel or CSV.

What counts towards the 5,000 records and 10 MB?

The records in all the tables of your database together, and its size including attachments. The sample database doesn't count.

Why can't I share on the free plan?

Sharing is what a licence is for. Preview as User opens your database exactly as your team would see it, so you can finish and test everything first.
