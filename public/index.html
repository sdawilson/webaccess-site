Questions

What does the free plan include?

One database of your own, up to 5,000 records and 10 MB, with every design tool: worksheets, tables, queries, forms, reports, macros and VBA. You also get the Fieldfare Traders sample to take apart. You sign in from one place at a time. Sharing a database with other people needs the Builder plan; Preview as User shows what they'd see.

What do I pay for?

Sharing. The Builder plan is £15 a month or £99 a year for each Admin, the people who design. Up to 10 people then use each database free, as Users or Guests. Pricing has the detail.

Is it Excel?

No. Web Access Builder is an independent product, and isn't made by or connected with Microsoft. Its workbook has several sheets, formulas and formatting, and it reads and writes Excel files, but it doesn't do everything Excel does. Coming from Excel says what carries over and what doesn't.

Is this Microsoft Access?

No. It's an independent product that works the way Access does, in a browser, without Access installed. Coming from Access has the detail.

Can I bring my workbook or my Access database?

Yes, as a starting point. A workbook imports with its values, formulas and formatting, and its data imports into tables. An Access database imports its tables, queries, forms, reports and code. Neither comes across perfectly: the Excel and Access pages say what to expect.

What happens to my data if I stop using it?

You can download a complete copy of a database at any time. A free account that isn't used for 180 days is deleted.

What does the AI assistant see?

The design of your database: its tables' fields, queries, forms, reports, macros and code. Not the records. If it would help to see a few rows of a table, it asks first, each time, and shows up to five if you allow it. It runs on Anthropic's Claude, only for Admins. Nothing it plans is built until you approve it.

Is there a demo?

Yes: demo.webaccessbuilder.com. One click gives you your own copy of the Fieldfare Traders sample with full design rights and no sign-up. It isn't kept: start a free account to keep what you build.
