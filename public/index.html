What you'll recognise from Access, and what changes

Watch it in use

Two and a half minutes, with narration and captions: a cell changed in a workbook and its record saved, a form running its VBA, a query and a report, a PivotTable and a PivotChart, the debugger stopping at a breakpoint, the assistant building a form, then a database shared and opened by a User.

VBA, Python and JavaScript: all here

Draw a form, drop controls on it, and choose the language for each event: a button's Click in Python, a text box's After Update in VBA, a key press in JavaScript. All three reach the same forms, data and commands. It runs in the browser, with nothing installed.

  • Event procedures in VBA, Python or JavaScript: Choose Builder asks which, and one form can mix all three
  • Python and JavaScript call the app as they run: MsgBox, DoCmd, recordsets, DLookup and your own VBA
  • VBA you know, moved on: class constructors, AndAlso, +=, Continue For and Return. Old code runs as it is
  • pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, SciPy and scikit-learn for Python, and libraries for statistics, dates and fuzzy matching for JavaScript, a tick away in References
  • Stored procedures in VBA: a server module runs on the server as one transaction, all or nothing
  • A code window with IntelliSense for VBA, breakpoints, stepping, Locals, Watches and the Immediate window

Questions

What does the free plan include?

One database of your own, up to 5,000 records and 10 MB, with every design tool: worksheets, tables, queries, forms, reports, macros, VBA, Python and JavaScript. You also get the Fieldfare Traders sample to take apart. You sign in from one place at a time. Sharing with other people needs Desktop or Server; Preview as User shows what they'd see.

What do I pay for?

Sharing, and you pay once. Desktop is £99: it runs on your own computer and shares on your office network. Server is £495: the web version on a server of your own. Each is for one Admin, the people who design. The people who use what you build pay nothing. Both are coming soon; Pricing has the detail.

Is it Excel?

No. Web Access Builder is an independent product, and isn't made by or connected with Microsoft. Its workbook has several sheets, formulas and formatting, and it reads and writes Excel files, but it doesn't do everything Excel does. Coming from Excel says what carries over and what doesn't.

Is this Microsoft Access?

No. It's an independent product that works the way Access does, in a browser, without Access installed. Coming from Access has the detail.

Can I bring my workbook or my Access database?

Yes, as a starting point. A workbook imports with its values, formulas and formatting, and its data imports into tables. An Access database imports its tables, queries, forms, reports and code. Neither comes across perfectly: the Excel and Access pages say what to expect.

What happens to my data if I stop using it?

You can download a complete copy of a database at any time. A free account that isn't used for 180 days is deleted.

Can I use Python or JavaScript?

Yes, as fully as VBA. Choose the language for each event: Choose Builder offers VBA, Python and JavaScript, and one form can mix them. Python and JavaScript call the app as they run, with message boxes, DoCmd, recordsets and your VBA procedures, and VBA can call them too. Tick the libraries you want in References: pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, SciPy and scikit-learn for Python; statistics, dates and fuzzy matching for JavaScript. They run in the browser, with nothing to install, and can't reach the network or your files. Python and JavaScript has the detail.

What does the AI assistant see?

The design of your database: its tables' fields, queries, forms, reports, macros and code. Not the records. If it would help to see a few rows of a table, it asks first, each time, and shows up to five if you allow it. It runs on Anthropic's Claude, only for Admins. Nothing it plans is built until you approve it.

Is there a demo?

Yes: demo.webaccessbuilder.com. One click gives you your own copy of the Fieldfare Traders sample with full design rights and no sign-up. It isn't kept: start a free account to keep what you build.
